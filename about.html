About GoalGear

Est. 2016 in London, GoalGear is a specialist football equipment store dedicated to providing players with authentic match balls, elite boots, and anti-slip grip socks. Our mission is to elevate your performance on the pitch with gear that meets professional standards.

Match & Training Balls

Every football in our catalogue undergoes strict sphericity, circumference, and rebound testing. From FIFA Quality Pro tournament balls to low-bounce futsal spheres, we guarantee true flight and air pressure retention.

Football Boots & Cleats

Engineered for speed, curve, and stability on grass, turf, and hardwood. We stock authentic Nike, Adidas, and Puma boots with anatomical collar lock-in and high-traction stud configurations.

Anti-Slip Grip Socks

Modern football demands zero slip inside your footwear. Our silicone-padded grip socks lock your feet firmly in place, eliminating blisters and maximizing explosive energy transfer during sprints.

Founded in London, 2016

Our Heritage & Quality Standard

GoalGear began with a simple observation: too many players were taking the pitch with poorly inflated footballs, ill-fitting footwear, and slipping socks that hampered their game.

Our founders set out to build a store dedicated entirely to the essential contact gear of the beautiful game. Over the last decade, we have partnered directly with manufacturers like Nike, Adidas, Puma, and Select to bring players authentic, pressure-calibrated gear.

Whether you are competing in a weekend league match, practicing freestyle footwork, or training at an academy, GoalGear is committed to elevating your performance.
