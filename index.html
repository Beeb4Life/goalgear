About GoalGear
Supplying Footballers & Academies Since 2016

GoalGear was founded by lifelong football enthusiasts dedicated exclusively to the most important piece of equipment in the game: the football. Whether you are competing under stadium floodlights, running youth academy drills on artificial turf, or playing 5-a-side on indoor courts, we curate verified, pressure-tested footballs from Adidas, Nike, Puma, Select, and legendary heritage manufacturers.

Every model in our catalogue is rigorously inspected for circumference consistency, sphericity, water absorption, and panel seam bonding.
